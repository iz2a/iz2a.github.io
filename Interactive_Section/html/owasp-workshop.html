OWASP Top 10 Workshop

Hands-on web exploitation against the most critical application risks. Each challenge is a small vulnerable app, you type the actual attack payload and watch it succeed or fail, capture the flag, and then read exactly why it worked and how to fix it. This is the offensive and defensive view in one place.

These techniques are for securing your own applications. Only test systems you are authorized to test.
How it works

Open a challenge, read the vulnerable code, type a payload in the field and hit Attack. A wrong payload gives a hint; a working one shows the result, the flag, and the remediation.
