# Strategy Development Policy
**Document Reference:** POL-1-1-1
**Version:** 1.0
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-10
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
**Approved By:** Board of Directors
**Next Review:** 2027-03-10
**Regulatory Reference:** NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC) v2.0 — Control 1-1-1

---

## 1. Purpose
The Strategy Development Policy exists to ensure that Your Organization has a comprehensive and aligned cybersecurity strategy that supports its overall business objectives, protects its information assets, and complies with the regulatory requirements of the Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA). This policy aims to establish a clear vision, objectives, and a 3-year roadmap for cybersecurity, thereby safeguarding the organization's digital infrastructure and data against evolving cyber threats.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors of Your Organization who have access to the organization's information systems, networks, and data. It covers all aspects of cybersecurity strategy development, implementation, and maintenance across the organization.

## 3. Policy Statement
The following policy statements are enforceable and apply to all personnel and systems within the scope of this policy:
1. **Cybersecurity Vision**: Your Organization will maintain a cybersecurity vision that aligns with its overall business strategy, focusing on the protection of information assets and the enablement of business operations through secure and resilient cyber systems.
2. **Objectives**: The organization will establish clear, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) objectives for its cybersecurity program, ensuring alignment with the NCA ECC v2.0 and other relevant regulatory frameworks.
3. **3-Year Roadmap**: A comprehensive 3-year roadmap will be developed and regularly updated to guide the implementation of the cybersecurity strategy, including key initiatives, milestones, and resource allocations.
4. **Review and Update**: The cybersecurity strategy, including the vision, objectives, and roadmap, will be reviewed and updated at least annually, or as needed, to ensure it remains relevant and effective in addressing emerging cyber threats and business requirements.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Develop, implement, and maintain the cybersecurity strategy, ensuring alignment with business objectives and regulatory requirements. |
| IT Department | Support the implementation of the cybersecurity strategy through the provision of secure and resilient IT systems and services. |
| Business Units | Collaborate with the CISO and IT Department to ensure that business requirements are integrated into the cybersecurity strategy and that units comply with cybersecurity policies and procedures. |
| Executive Management | Approve the cybersecurity strategy and provide necessary resources for its implementation, ensuring that cybersecurity is a priority at all levels of the organization. |

## 5. Implementation Requirements
To comply with NCA ECC v2.0 Control 1-1-1, the following specific requirements must be met:
- Develop a cybersecurity strategy document that outlines the vision, objectives, and 3-year roadmap.
- Ensure the strategy is aligned with the organization's overall business strategy and objectives.
- Establish a governance framework that outlines roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes for cybersecurity.
- Allocate necessary resources (financial, human, technological) to support the implementation of the cybersecurity strategy.

## 6. Compliance Monitoring
Compliance with this policy will be monitored through:
- Annual reviews of the cybersecurity strategy to ensure it remains aligned with business objectives and regulatory requirements.
- Quarterly progress reports on the implementation of the 3-year roadmap.
- Regular audits and risk assessments to identify gaps in the cybersecurity posture and ensure that corrective actions are taken.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy must be approved by the CISO and Executive Management. The exception process involves:
- Submitting a formal request for exception, including justification and proposed alternative solutions.
- Review and approval by the CISO and Executive Management, considering the potential risks and impacts on the organization's cybersecurity posture.

## 8. Violations and Consequences
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary actions, up to and including termination of employment or contract, depending on the severity of the violation. Consequences may also include:
- Mandatory cybersecurity awareness training for individuals found to be in violation.
- Implementation of additional security controls or measures to mitigate identified risks.
- Reporting of significant violations to regulatory authorities as required by law.
