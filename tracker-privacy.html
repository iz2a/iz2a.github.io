Visitor Analytics Notice

Last updated: November 2026

This website uses a first-party analytics tool that I built and run myself, so I can see how the site is used and know when someone visits. No third-party advertising or analytics companies are involved, and your data is not sold or shared.

What is collected

  • Your IP address and the approximate location and network (country, city, internet provider) derived from it
  • The pages you view, how you arrived (referrer), time on page and scroll depth
  • Your browser and device: user agent, screen size, language and timezone
  • A random identifier stored in your browser so repeat visits are recognised

What is not done

No device fingerprinting, no attempt to re-identify you after you clear your browser storage, and no attempt to detect or defeat a VPN. If you clear your cookies and site data, you are counted as a brand new visitor.

Why

To understand which content is useful, where visitors come from, and to be notified of visits. This is my legitimate interest as the site owner.

Retention and your rights

Data is kept for a limited period and then deleted. Under Saudi PDPL and similar laws you may request access to, or deletion of, data relating to you. Contact me through the details on the home page.

You can opt out of analytics on this browser. Your browser's Global Privacy Control signal is also honoured automatically.
