# ApplierPilotAI Script

**Intro**

Hello there. I'm Matthew, I'm Aziz, and I'm Gleb. This is ApplierPilotAI: an AI-powered platform designed to streamline the job application process. We built this tool to help job seekers create optimized resumes, personalized cover letters, and prepare confidently for interviews, all with the help of AI tools.

Applying for jobs can be very time consuming and overwhelming, and many qualified candidates often get overlooked due to their lacking and unoptimized application materials and preparedness. Our solution is a website that provides AI tooling that helps users optimize their resumes, cover letters, interview readiness, and more. This way, job candidates can put their best foot forward without needing to pay for a professional job coach. 

**Features**

Here are many of the features that we worked hard on:

- **Profile dashboard**: once logged in, you are able to navigate to a page in which you can manage your profile. You have the ability to edit your basic information, modify your job search preferences to suit your needs, and upload your resume, so that our various AI tools can offer personalized feedback and critique

- **AI Resume optimizer**: once you upload a resume, you have the option to gain personalized feedback on it. Our resume optimizer uses AI to analyze a candidate's resume and provide honest feedback on structure, phrasing, missing keywords, and more suggestions so that candidates can further polish their resume, highlight key achievements, and get past ATS systems 

- **Job dashboard**: we also have a fully fledged job dashboard, where users can browse various jobs, with the ability to search for jobs containing keywords, and filter jobs by location and industry. Each of these job cards offers many different actionable features that we can try. Let's start with the interview coach

- **AI Interview coach**: the interview coach tool essentially takes the user's resume (if they have one) and the job details, and uses that data to generate 5 interview questions that a candidate might possibly see in an interview. Each of these questions prompts a response from the user. Once the user submits their response, the AI analyzes their response and provides critical feedback on the candidate's answer. Additionally, users have the option to regenerate 5 completely new questions, if they want more practice. This is a very useful feature for those who are looking to prepare for an interview for a specific position

- **AI Cover letter generator**: we also have another tool called the cover letter generator. Using the job details, the candidate's resume, and the candidate's personal information if they choose to enter it, the AI tool will generate a personalized cover letter finely tuned for the corresponding job position. This is a convenient aid for those looking to go the extra mile with a cover letter

- **AI Fit analysis**: the fit analysis tool reads the job details and the candidate's resume, and uses that to generate a full analysis on how good of a fit the candidate is for the particular job position, while providing useful feedback. This is particularly good for finding jobs that closely match the candidate's aspirations and qualifications

- **AI Rejection simulator**: we have another tool called the rejection simulator, which essentially uses the job details to generate five of the most likely reasons that the candidate would get rejected, additionally using the candidate's resume if they'd like. This is useful for finding weakspots in a user's resume if they are interested in landing a particular position 

- **Applications**: if we click on the apply button, it takes us to a page which is intended to prepare us for the application, using the aforementioned features. First, it offers to tune your resume based on the job details, then drafts a cover letter using the cover letter generator, and then generates a fit analysis. Once the user completes these (if they wish), they are now ready to apply! Clicking here will take the user to the external job posting, which they can submit their application, now with much more preparedness and tuning

- **Job recommendations**: in addition to the job dashboard, if we navigate to our profile, the user can also use the job recommendations tool to find jobs that suit them based on their prefences that they entered in their profile. If the user uploads a resume, our backend AI will read the resume and base the job search off of its details as well as profile description. 

- **Application history**: lastly, we have a system to track the jobs that the user has both viewed and applied to, so that users have the ability to see their applications progress, and also to ensure that they don't accidentally apply to the same place again

**Biggest Challenge**

We would like to address one piece of information that was critical to the development of the project: our biggest challenge.

The biggest obstacle we faced in this project was having to pivot it from its initial purpose. Essentially, the original idea of the platform was a tool that automatically applies to job boards such as LinkedIn. However, over halfway through the semester, we realized that what we were building would not be feasible. Selenium on the server side for a multitude of users was going to cause problems and simply not doable. Also, LinkedIn's TOS does not allow for third parties to send automatic applications, so if we continued on our path, our project would inevitably become dysfunctional after LinkedIn takes action. This was an unfortunate dilemma, but nevertheless, we didn't let that stop us. Instead of automatically applying to jobs, we repurposed the platform to be aimed towards improving candidates' applications and increasing preparedness. Ultimately, we're proud of what we've created despite having to pivot, and we believe that we made the right choice.
