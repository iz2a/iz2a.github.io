About

I am an IT and Information Security Senior Consultant at ECOVIS Saudi Arabia (ECOVIS Al Sabti) in Riyadh. I deliver risk-based compliance engagements for some of the Kingdom's most high-profile organizations, including Aramco Digital, American Express (AESA), Saudi Public Security and IMCTC, across SAMA CSF/MVC/CTI, NCA ECC/DCC and ITGC.

Research

Where cybersecurity, digital forensics and criminal justice meet, with a focus on Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

  1. Post-Quantum Cryptography for Critical Infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and the GCC

    Benchmarks the NIST post-quantum standards (ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA) against classical algorithms on an Apple M2 Pro and calculates the cost of post-quantum message sizes on industrial protocols such as DNP3 and Modbus. On capable hardware the obstacles are bandwidth and embedded firmware trust anchors, not computation. Maps a migration roadmap onto NCA and SAMA frameworks; all results are reproducible.

    Working paper, 2026ML-KEMICS / OT
    Read PDF

  2. Quantum Cryptography and Digital Evidence in Criminal Investigations: A Saudi Perspective

    Reviews how post-quantum cryptography changes digital forensics and what it means for evidence under Saudi Arabia's Law of Evidence (2022) and Anti-Cyber Crime Law. A small reproducible experiment shows ML-KEM secret keys remain recoverable from process memory during use but not after secure erasure, which is why live forensics matters in the post-quantum era.

    Working paper, 2026Memory forensicsSaudi law
    Read PDF

  3. Forensic Analysis of Cryptocurrency-Based Ransomware: Technical and Criminal-Justice Perspectives

    How ransomware operations use cryptocurrency for payment and laundering, the blockchain-tracing techniques used to investigate them, and the criminal-justice challenges that follow. Grounded in public cases such as the 2021 Colonial Pipeline seizure and published on-chain data, with a regional section on Saudi Arabia and the GCC.
